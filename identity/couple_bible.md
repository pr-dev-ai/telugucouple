# Satyam & Padma — character bible v1 (3 Nov 2026)

Fully fictional. Not based on any real person's face, photos or voice. The account carries Instagram's AI label.
The machine-readable copy of every text here is `tools/characters.py`; the frozen faces are `identity/<who>/v1`.

## The couple
- **Satyam** (S. Satyam), 45. Completely bald crown with short black hair at the sides, thick black moustache, plain round face, paunch. Educated, a section officer at a government office in Kukatpally; the flat is his own. Plain and unstylish, never grey or old: at home a faded T-shirt or banian and lungi; from the office an old-fashioned ironed checked shirt tucked in with the belt above the belly, bathroom chappals. Fit enough to dance; dances every step stiff and wrong with full josh. The point: he is the total opposite of her glamour and figure, and she still adores him.
- **Padma** (P. Padma), 28. Beautiful: round soft face, dimples, big kajal eyes, long plait with jasmine, round maroon bindi, mangalsutra, bangles. Curvy figure, fuller bust, medium waist, wide hips. Runs a small boutique/tailoring business from the spare bedroom. Loves her husband openly and teases him on camera; she is the one who drags him into every trending dance and does the steps properly.
- Married 5 years, no kids yet, arranged marriage that became a love story. Her line: "Ayana ni chudandi" (look at him). His line: "Inkosari? Sare." (Again? Fine.)

## The flat (every reel is shot here, nowhere else)
Neat upper-middle-class 3BHK in a gated community, Kukatpally (KPHB), Hyderabad. Light cream walls, glossy beige tiles, false ceiling with warm LED cove light, tidy modern Indian interior, a real family home (not a showroom). Rooms and their plates in `sets/`:
- **hall_a / hall_b**: grey L-shaped sofa, big wall TV on a wooden unit, glass centre table, tall plant, sheer curtains on a big window, brass Venkateswara idol on a shelf. Most dance reels (floor space).
- **dining_a**: six-seater table, pendant lamp, crockery unit, arch to the kitchen.
- **bedroom_a / bedroom_b**: queen bed with a cream quilt, full-wall wardrobe with a mirror panel, split AC, bedside lamps.
- **kitchen_a**: modular white-and-wood kitchen, chimney, four-burner hob, pressure cooker, microwave, purifier.
- **balcony_a**: steel-and-glass railing, potted plants, tulasi, cane chair, swing chair, gated-community towers behind.
- **door_a**: entrance foyer, teak door with the brass nameplate "S. Satyam", shoe cabinet, mirror with key holder, tulasi pot.
Lighting rule: evening = warm LED cove light + lamp; morning = daylight through sheer curtains; night = dim warm light. Clean and neat, but a phone video, never cinematic.

## What the account is about
Trending Telugu folk/DJ dance reels, 13–16 s, both of them in frame, full-body steps, inside the flat. The joke is always the same and never gets old: she is graceful and glamorous, he is a badly dressed uncle doing the steps wrong with total commitment. Occasional kitchen/balcony bits with the same pair. No gym, no travel, no outdoor (that is Siri's account).

## How they talk (captions, comments)
Telangana Telugu mixed with English, Kukatpally middle-class register. Captions in Padma's voice. Short lines.
- "Ayana steps chudandi 😂 #<song>" / "Maa intlo daily idi" / "Uncle ki full josh, steps zero" / "Tag the couple who dance like this at every function"
- About being AI: platform label on every post; bio says it once; captions and hashtags do not repeat "AI" (same rule as Siri).

## Never
- Never a real person's face, name or voice. No politics, no religion debates, no body shaming (the joke is on his dancing, never on his looks in a cruel way), no medical or diet claims.
- Padma's glamour stays in the saree register that Instagram has been rewarding on Siri's account: deep-neck blouse, waist showing, full-body dance framing. No chest close-ups, no swimwear, no underwear: the Reels tab (97% of reach) demotes "sexually suggestive" reels and that kills the account.
- No brand mention without the Paid Partnership tag.
